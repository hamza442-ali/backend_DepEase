Introduction                                                                 
The Automated Final Year Project (FYP) Management System is designed to enhance the presentation, management, and accessibility of final year projects for educational institutions. By leveraging cloud-based technologies and Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines, this system aims to provide a seamless and efficient platform for students, teachers, and other stakeholders.

Problem Statement                                                
The current methodology for presenting Final Year Projects (FYPs) by students involves running them on Local host. However, this approach presents a substantial challenge when it comes to later accessibility for teachers or other stakeholders. As a result, when there's a need to review, evaluate, or share these projects at a later stage, teachers encounter significant difficulties in accessing and assessing the work effectively. This limitation hampers the ability of educational institutions to maintain a comprehensive repository of FYPs and impedes the potential for cross-referencing, archiving, and knowledge sharing. While existing solutions like Jira offer automation capabilities, their complex interfaces and high costs are not feasible for educational institutions. Moreover, these solutions are designed for large-scale enterprises, not tailored to the specific needs of universities. There is a clear need for an automated FYP management system specifically designed for educational institutions.

Proposed Solution                                        
Introducing an innovative solution to address the challenges of presenting and managing Final Year Projects (FYPs), we propose the development of a user-friendly and cost-effective Automated FYP Management System. This system will streamline the process by implementing a Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipeline, eliminating the reliance on local hosts. Students' FYPs will undergo automated testing, building, and deployment to a secure cloud-based platform. This approach ensures easy, on-demand accessibility to deployed project versions for teachers and stakeholders. The system's intuitive interface will enable students to submit their projects effortlessly, triggering the automated CI/CD pipeline. Additionally, teachers can review, evaluate, and provide feedback in real-time through the cloud-hosted instances. By catering specifically to educational institutions, this solution bridges the accessibility gap, promotes efficient archiving, and enables seamless cross-referencing and knowledge sharing. With its tailored design and affordability, the Automated FYP Management System empowers universities to maintain a comprehensive repository while facilitating effective project assessment and fostering collaborative learning.

Features                                     
Automated CI/CD Pipeline: Automates the process of testing, building, and deploying FYPs to a cloud-based platform.                        
User-Friendly Interface: Intuitive design for easy submission, review, and management of projects.                                    
Real-Time Feedback: Allows teachers to provide immediate feedback and evaluations.                                  
Secure Cloud Hosting: Ensures projects are securely hosted and accessible on demand.                                                    
Comprehensive Repository: Maintains an organized and searchable database of all FYPs.                                                                                  
Cross-Referencing and Knowledge Sharing: Facilitates easy cross-referencing between projects and promotes knowledge sharing among students and faculty.                                             
Separate Panels for Teacher, Students and Administration where they have modules like Progress tracking, Module and Deliverables, Propsals, Evaluation etc                         

Benefits                              
Improved Accessibility: Projects are easily accessible from anywhere at any time.                     
Streamlined Workflow: Simplifies the process of submitting, reviewing, and managing FYPs.                         
Cost-Effective: Tailored for educational institutions with affordability in mind.                  
Enhanced Collaboration: Promotes collaborative learning and knowledge sharing.                                    
Efficient Archiving: Maintains a well-organized repository of projects for future reference.
